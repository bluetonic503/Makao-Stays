Food guide

A taste of Nairobi

Nairobi's food scene reflects Kenya's many cultures and its international community. Try nyama choma (grilled meat) with ugali and kachumbari, Swahili coastal dishes like pilau and biryani, and Kenyan breakfast staples such as chapati and mandazi with chai. Parklands is famous for Indian food, Westlands for international restaurants and nightlife, and Karen for relaxed garden dining.

Kenya also grows some of the world's most celebrated coffee — Nairobi's cafés are a great place to taste it.
