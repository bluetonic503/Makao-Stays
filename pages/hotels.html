Guide

Choosing a hotel in Nairobi

Hotels in Westlands are ideal for business travel and nightlife, with quick access to JKIA via the Expressway. Upper Hill hotels serve the corporate district and major hospitals, while Karen offers boutique, garden-style hotels close to the Giraffe Centre and Nairobi National Park. Travellers on a budget often choose the CBD or Parklands.

What to look for

  • Airport transfers — useful for late-night arrivals at JKIA.
  • Breakfast included — common in Nairobi hotels.
  • Backup power and reliable Wi-Fi for remote work.
  • Secure parking if you'll be renting a car.

Frequently asked questions

Which area is best for hotels in Nairobi?

Westlands is the most popular all-round choice. Upper Hill suits business travellers, Karen suits relaxed holidays, and the CBD offers budget options close to the city's sights.

Do Nairobi hotels offer airport pick-up?

Many do on request, usually for an extra fee. Ask when you send your booking request.
