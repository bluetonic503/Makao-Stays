1. Who we are

Makao Stays (“we”, “us”) operates this website to help people discover and request accommodation and restaurants in Nairobi, Kenya. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you send us — for example your name, phone number, email, travel dates, number of guests and messages, when you contact us via WhatsApp, email or a website form.
  • Property owner details — when you submit a property for listing.
  • Technical data — standard server logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and browser type).

The website itself has no user accounts and does not collect payment card details.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and process booking requests.
  • To check availability with property owners and share the details needed for your booking.
  • To verify properties and communicate with owners.
  • To send newsletters, only if you subscribe (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing your information

We share booking details with the relevant property owner or manager only to arrange your stay. We do not sell your personal data. Messages sent via WhatsApp are processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own privacy policy.

5. Cookies & local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website uses your browser's session storage to remember your search dates and guests while you browse; this stays on your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

6. Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare), images from Unsplash and maps from OpenStreetMap. These providers may receive your IP address when content loads.

7. Data retention

We keep enquiry and booking records only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
