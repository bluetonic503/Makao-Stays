How onboarding works

  1. Submit your details

    Use the form — it takes about two minutes.

  2. Verification

    We confirm your ownership or authority to rent, and check the property matches its description.

  3. Photos & listing

    We prepare your listing with accurate photos, amenities and house rules.

  4. Go live

    Your property is published as “Verified” and starts receiving requests.

Commission or listing fees, if any, are agreed in writing during onboarding.
