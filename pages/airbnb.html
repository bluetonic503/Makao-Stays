Short stay apartments in Nairobi

Short-stay apartments are a great alternative to hotels: you get a kitchen, more space and often self check-in. Kilimani and Westlands are the most popular areas for short stays, while Syokimau is convenient for early flights from JKIA.

Makao Stays is an independent booking service and is not affiliated with Airbnb. “Airbnb-style” describes the type of stay.

Frequently asked questions

Is a short stay cheaper than a hotel?

Often, especially for groups and longer stays, because you can self-cater and share space.

Can I book a single night?

Many short stays accept one night; each listing shows its minimum stay.
