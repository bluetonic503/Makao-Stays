Airport transfer FAQs

How far is JKIA from central Nairobi?

JKIA is roughly 15–20 km south-east of the city centre. Depending on traffic and route, the drive to Westlands or Kilimani typically takes 25–75 minutes.

What's the difference between JKIA and Wilson Airport?

JKIA handles international and most domestic airline flights. Wilson Airport, on Langata Road, mainly serves light aircraft, charters and safari flights.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you request a transfer so the driver can track your arrival time.
