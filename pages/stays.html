Guide

Affordable to luxury accommodation in Nairobi

Nairobi offers accommodation for every budget and trip length — from budget hotels in the CBD and Parklands, to serviced apartments in Westlands and Gigiri, to private villas in Karen and Runda. Short-stay apartments in Kilimani and Kileleshwa are popular with visitors who want a kitchen and more space, while monthly furnished rentals suit people relocating to Kenya.

Accommodation for foreigners in Nairobi

International visitors usually choose neighbourhoods with good security, reliable Wi-Fi and easy airport access. Westlands, Kilimani, Gigiri and Upper Hill are all well connected to JKIA, especially via the Nairobi Expressway.
