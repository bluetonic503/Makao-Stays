About us

Welcome to Makao Stays

“Makao” means home in Swahili. We help visitors find a comfortable, safe place to call home while they're in Nairobi.

Our mission

Trustworthy stays, booked the easy way

Finding accommodation in a new city is stressful — especially from abroad. Listings can be outdated, photos misleading and payment requests suspicious. We built Makao Stays to make booking in Nairobi simple and honest.

Every booking request goes to a real person on WhatsApp who checks availability before any money changes hands. We're clear about what's confirmed and what isn't, and we never invent reviews or ratings.

  • International visitors, tourists and business travellers
  • Students, interns and people relocating to Kenya
  • Local property owners who want to reach more guests

How we work

Our listing standards

We verify first

A property is marked “Verified” only after we've confirmed it exists, the owner or manager is authorised to rent it, and it matches its description.

Samples are labelled

While we onboard properties, demonstration listings are clearly marked “Sample” and use illustrative stock photos.

No fake partnerships

We don't claim partnerships with hotels, restaurants or platforms unless they genuinely exist.
