Guide

Luxury accommodation in Nairobi for families and groups

Nairobi's leafy suburbs — Karen, Runda and Lavington — are known for spacious homes with mature gardens. Villas with private pools are popular with families and groups, while guest houses offer a warm, personal welcome at lower prices.

Frequently asked questions

Are villas suitable for events?

Only with the owner's prior written approval. Most properties do not allow parties.

Is a security deposit required?

Some larger homes and villas require a refundable deposit, confirmed before payment.
