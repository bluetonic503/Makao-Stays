Guide

Serviced vs furnished apartments

Serviced apartments include housekeeping and often a gym, pool and reception — popular in Westlands and Gigiri. Furnished apartments are fully equipped homes, popular in Kilimani, Kileleshwa and South B. Long-term rentals are priced per month and suit people relocating to Nairobi, students and contract workers.

Furnished apartments in Kilimani

Kilimani is one of Nairobi's most popular areas for furnished apartments thanks to its central location, cafés and easy access to Upper Hill and the CBD.
