1. Booking steps

  1. Search for accommodation and open a listing.
  2. Choose your check-in and check-out dates and number of guests.
  3. Tap Book Now — WhatsApp opens with your booking request pre-filled. Press send.
  4. We confirm availability with the property owner or manager.
  5. We send you the final price and payment instructions on WhatsApp.
  6. Your booking is confirmed only after the required verification and payment.

2. Booking disclaimer

Tapping Book Now does not confirm a booking. Availability, final price and payment instructions must be confirmed by Makao Stays or the property owner. Until you receive written confirmation, the property is not reserved for you.

3. Payment safety

  • Payment instructions are only sent from our official WhatsApp number: .
  • We never ask you to pay before availability is confirmed.
  • Check that the payee details match those we send you. If in doubt, ask us before paying.
  • Keep your payment confirmation and booking messages.

4. Sample listings

Listings marked “Sample” are for demonstration only and cannot be booked as real stays.

5. Changes & cancellations

Cancellation terms vary by property and are shared before you pay. Contact us as early as possible if your plans change.
