Airport transfers Nairobi travel guide

Before you fly

Most visitors must apply online for an Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA). Use only the official government portal and check the current rules.

Getting around

Ride-hailing apps are the easiest way to travel. The Nairobi Expressway links JKIA with Westlands and helps avoid traffic.

Money & SIM cards

Cards work in malls and hotels, but mobile money (M-Pesa) is everywhere. Bring your passport to register a local SIM.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book accommodation in Nairobi with Makao Stays?

Find a stay you like, choose your dates and number of guests, then tap Book Now. WhatsApp opens with your booking request already written — just press send. We'll confirm availability and send payment instructions.

Is my booking confirmed when I tap Book Now?

No. Tapping Book Now only sends a booking request. Your booking is confirmed after we have checked availability with the property and your payment has been verified.

Which currency will I pay in?

Prices are set in Kenyan shillings (KES). US dollar amounts are shown as approximate conversions to help you compare. The exact amount and accepted payment methods are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can you arrange an airport pick-up from JKIA?

Many properties can arrange airport transfers on request. Mention it in your booking request, or use our airport transfer request form.

Which Nairobi neighbourhood is best for first-time visitors?

Westlands and Kilimani are popular for their central locations, restaurants and nightlife; Karen suits relaxed holidays near wildlife attractions; Gigiri is convenient for UN and embassy visits. See our neighbourhood guides.
